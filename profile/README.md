**Diskling is a native Mac app that tells you why your disk is full and gets the space back safely.**

## What Diskling does

- **Explains the space.** One plain sentence about what is filling your disk, instead of a vague "System Data" bar.
- **Finds what Finder hides.** Old iOS simulators, Docker images, package caches, leftovers from deleted apps, and cloud files stored on your Mac.
- **Removes things safely.** Files go to the Trash first, and developer tools are cleaned with their own commands.
- **Uninstalls apps completely.** Apps and everything they leave behind.
- **Warns you before the disk fills up.** So your Mac never freezes from a full disk.

Free to try for 14 days, and Free keeps working after that. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription. Mac only.
